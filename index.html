🎓 Student Segmentation Explorer

Look up a student’s academic profile and explore the K-means segment formed from grades, study time, absences, and prior failures.

Demo data is fictional. Use this page to explore the project, not to make judgments or high-stakes decisions about students. Do not publish real students’ names or grades on a public website.

Data and settings

CSV needs a student_name column and at least two numeric columns. Uploaded data stays in this browser tab.
